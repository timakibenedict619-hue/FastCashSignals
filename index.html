Understanding Bitcoin

Learn about Bitcoin, blockchain technology, scarcity, decentralization, and the risks involved in digital assets.

What is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is a decentralized digital currency created in 2009. It operates through a peer-to-peer network rather than relying on a central bank.

Bitcoin transactions are recorded on a public blockchain, allowing the network to verify transactions without a central authority.

Fixed Supply

Bitcoin has a protocol-defined maximum supply of 21 million coins.

Decentralized

Bitcoin is maintained by a distributed network of participants around the world.

Transparent

Blockchain transactions can be independently verified by participants.

How Bitcoin Works

A simple overview of how Bitcoin transactions move through the network.

1

Transaction

A Bitcoin transaction is created and signed by the sender.

2

Broadcast

The transaction is broadcast to the Bitcoin network.

3

Verification

Network participants verify the transaction and include it in a block.

4

Confirmation

Once confirmed, the transaction becomes part of the blockchain's permanent record.
